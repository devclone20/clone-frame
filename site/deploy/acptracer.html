01 · Connect

Connect your wallet, your key, your host

Non-custodial by design. Your seed and your LLM key never touch our servers — the wallet stays in your browser, the key stays local. Pick where the agent runs: your own machine, or a droplet the Harness sets up for you.

◈Owner wallet

Sign in with your wallet (WalletConnect / Base smart-wallet / a 6551 token-bound account). SIWE proves ownership — no key leaves the browser.

Connected: · SIWE verified (ERC-6492/1271 aware).

⚿Your LLM key byo · live

Bring your own model key — stored client-side only, never sent to us. Anthropic works directly from the browser; OpenAI often needs a local model or proxy (CORS).

▤Where it runs

Your own machine for full control, or a droplet to stay online 24/7 hunting jobs. The Harness writes the exact setup either way.

  • Agent runs as a supervised service (launchd/systemd), not a fragile terminal tab.
  • Close safely anytime — the Harness quiesces, drains, and writes update.md so nothing breaks.
  • Step-by-step to a DigitalOcean droplet (or any server) — the Harness does the hardening.
  • Coordinator stays online in bootstrapper mode, hunting ACP jobs while you sleep.

06 · Harness · watchdog · the four gates

The harness with eyes and hands

ATLAS's harness core — inherited, not forked. It proposes; it never signs. Four gates sit between intent and money, and none of them can collapse.

⬡Harnesses named · switchable

Each harness is a named team of agents with its own soul, crew and gates. Operate as one, or add your own — the Terminal runs as the active harness.

◇ ATLAS Harness + Add harness
ATLAS crew · 9 roles

The brain proposes, the gates dispose, the signer signs — no LLM in the signing path.

⚖Committee-Evaluator Gate · ≥2

No deliverable ships until at least two independent evaluators agree under quorum. Quality is enforced, not hoped for.

⛔Safety veto Gate · every byte

Screens every outbound byte. One veto stops the send. Never collapses.

▤Policy Gate Gate · caps · allowlist

Deterministic code: spend caps, address allowlist, per-wallet fenced lease. No prompt can override it.

✋Owner HITL Gate · above threshold

Anything above your threshold waits for you. The Harness supervises the first four interactions live.
